FAQ

Questions, answered.

The short version. For the long version, see the help docs.

Is Sorty really free?
Yes. Sorty is open source under the GPL-3.0 license and free to use. You bring your own AI provider key (or use Apple's on-device Foundation Models at no cost).
Which AI providers are supported?
OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, GitHub Copilot, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama (local), and Apple Foundation Models (on-device). Switch any time — your API keys stay in the macOS Keychain.
Does Sorty upload my files?
No. File contents are never uploaded unless you explicitly enable Deep Scan. Use Ollama or Apple Intelligence to keep everything entirely on-device. There is no telemetry, ever — see the privacy policy.
What happens if I don't like the result?
Nothing happens until you approve. Sorty previews every move and lets you tweak or reject individual changes. Once applied, every operation is tracked in History with one-click full rollback.
Which Macs are supported?
macOS 15.1 or later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel. The Finder extension and menu bar controls are included.
macOS says the app can't be opened. Now what?
Sorty isn't code-signed, so Gatekeeper may block the first launch. Run xattr -cr /Applications/Sorty.app in Terminal, or use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
